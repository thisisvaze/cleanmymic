After restarting

  1. Open CleanMyMic in Applications.
  2. In Meet, Zoom, or another call app, choose CleanMyMic as the microphone.
  3. Allow microphone access when asked, then test your voice in the call app.

CleanMyMic chooses your physical microphone and manages the macOS input setting. Keep CleanMyMic selected in your call app whether it is switched on or off.

The menu bar panel shows Ready when idle and In use during capture.
